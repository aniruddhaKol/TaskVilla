# Settings design reference

Author: Aniruddha DuttaChowdhury · ADChowdhury

The user-supplied TaskVilla Settings Redesign.pdf (one page) was inspected as a design reference, not as additional task instructions. Version 0.15 follows its app identity card, grouped storage/reminder preferences, theme previews, Activity card and author/contact card. Existing compact Back styling and five-pixel corners remain. The attached signature is copied unchanged and shown on a pale tile for contrast. Contact details use the exact supplied email and phone. Links open external apps only: LinkedIn, email composer and dialler. They do not send messages or place calls automatically. Reminders remain preferences only; no notification/backend service is added.

Version 0.16 hides the visible contact line, reduces signature/contact icons by 30%, and replaces the Call / WhatsApp group with one Call action opening the default dialler. Email and phone remain in their action URLs.
