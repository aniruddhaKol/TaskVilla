# TaskVilla emoji catalogue

Author: Aniruddha DuttaChowdhury · ADChowdhury

Version 0.19 bundles 3,963 fully-qualified Unicode Emoji 18.0 entries derived from https://unicode.org/Public/emoji/latest/emoji-test.txt (source date 2026-04-30). Catalogue source is bundled as generated assets/emoji-catalogue.js, with original Unicode licence in assets/Unicode-LICENSE.txt. The picker requires no internet connection. Emoji glyph artwork is rendered by device fonts; new Unicode sequences may display differently or lack a glyph on older devices.

Search covers official emoji names, subgroup labels, category names and exact emoji sequences. Category selection narrows results. Batches of 120 can be expanded through Show more; no catalogue entries are hidden by a fixed shortlist. Country flags and skin-tone variants are selectable. Direct keyboard entry remains available.
